06 — FAQ

Honest answers.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. The whole point of the workshop is that you'll never touch a line of code. Claude does the writing, the design, the layout, and the deploy. You bring the taste.

What if I'm not a designer?

Most of the cohort isn't. Marketers, founders, operators — anyone who has ever waited 6 weeks for an agency or stared at a Figma file wondering what to do next.

Will I actually have a shipped page by the end?

Yes. By 1:30 PM ET, your page is on a live URL. We don't end early.

Can I attend if I'm not in the US?

Yes — it's Zoom. You'll get the recording the next day if your timezone is brutal.

What's the refund policy?

Full refund up to 7 days before. After that, you get the recording and a credit for the next cohort.
